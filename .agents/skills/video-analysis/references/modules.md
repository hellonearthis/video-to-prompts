# Form-specific modules

Load a section only after the first pass shows the video matches that form. These tell you what to extract; they do not tell you what the video is. If a video mixes forms, use more than one.

Contents: Procedural · Conversational · Narrative · Screen recording and gameplay · Music and performance · Advertising and promotional · News and documentary · Short-form social · Animation and virtual · Surveillance-style

## Procedural (tutorials, demos, recipes, repairs, workouts)
Goal of the procedure; tools, materials, prerequisites; ordered steps with timestamps; quantities, settings, timings, repetitions; safety warnings; mistakes and corrections shown; before and after states; critical actions that determine success; ambiguous instructions that need clarification.

## Conversational (interviews, podcasts, meetings, debates)
Participants and roles (labels unless named in the media); turn-taking and interruptions; positions, agreements, disagreements, unresolved points; claims versus evidence versus opinion versus speculation; tone shifts as observed cues; decisions, action items, deadlines.

## Narrative (films, trailers, vlogs, scripted content)
Premise, setting, characters, conflict, turning points, resolution, themes; narrative perspective and reliability; foreshadowing, callbacks, reveals, ellipsis, nonlinear order; how sound, editing, color, and camera support mood. Separate story-world events from presentation choices.

## Screen recording and gameplay
Platform, app, window, tabs, menus, cursor, dialogs, status indicators; user actions in order (click, type, scroll, select, submit, navigate); visible URLs, filenames, error messages, code, settings, values; loading states, latency, unexpected behavior. For games: player, enemies, objectives, HUD, score, health, resources, map location, match phase.

## Music and performance
Performers, instruments, stage, audience, choreography, costume, lighting; music-to-image synchronization; cuts on beat; indicators of live versus playback and lip-sync; visual motifs; mood and genre cues as described features, not intent.

## Advertising and promotional
Product, brand (only if legible or stated), claimed value, audience cues, offers, prices, calls to action, disclaimers, testimonials, demonstrations; persuasion techniques as observed (social proof, scarcity, authority, before/after, comparison, endorsement); possibly misleading claims, omitted conditions, small-print legibility.

## News and documentary
Source labels, dates, locations, interviewees, narration, b-roll, archival material, maps, graphics, chyrons; separate reporting, commentary, testimony, and editorial framing; whether visuals support spoken claims; missing context, selective editing, unclear attribution. Do not verify outside facts unless other evidence is provided.

## Short-form social
Hook in the first seconds, pacing, captions, audio source cues, meme or template format, overlays, calls to action; whether the clip appears excerpted; engagement-bait or sensational framing as observed features.

## Animation and virtual content
Style (2D, 3D, stop motion, pixel art, motion graphics, game engine, generated); rigging, lip-sync, physics, camera simulation, continuity; whether it is presented as real or fictional.

## Surveillance-style
See `safety-privacy.md`. Extract camera viewpoint, overlay timestamps, entries and exits, object and vehicle movement, anomalous events, and quality limits. Report movement, not suspected intent.
