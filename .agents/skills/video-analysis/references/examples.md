# Worked examples: weak versus strong output

## 1. Observation mixed with inference

**Weak:** "The man is angry and storms out of the room, clearly furious at his boss."
Problems: emotion asserted, relationship assumed, intent assumed.

**Strong:** "At 00:42 a man in a grey jacket stands, pushes his chair back, and walks out of frame left; the chair tips over (visual · observed). His voice is raised in the preceding sentence (audio · observed). Anger is one reading; frustration or urgency would fit equally (inferred · possible). Who the other person is and what the relationship is cannot be determined (unknown)."

## 2. Text and speech

**Weak:** "The narrator explains that the product is the best on the market."
**Strong:** "From 00:15 a voiceover says: 'This is the one you've been waiting for' (audio · observed). On-screen text at 00:16-00:19 reads 'LIMITED OFFER' (partially legible: second line illegible)."

## 3. Imported frame

**Weak:** "The rapid cuts show channel-surfing through analog TV static."
(Assumes a frame the media does not establish.)
**Strong:** "Between 00:20 and 00:26 the picture changes six times, each segment from a different visual source, with a brief noise texture between some changes (visual · observed). This may indicate deliberate segmentation; a simple compilation without a unifying concept is also consistent with the evidence (hypothesis · possible)."

## 4. Direct question

**User:** "Does anyone say the company name?"
**Strong:** "No company name is spoken in the audio I could transcribe. A logo reading 'ACME' is visible bottom right from 00:03 to 00:10 (text · observed). Speech between 00:31 and 00:40 is unclear, so I can't rule out a spoken mention there."

## 5. Limited input

**User uploads a video; only frames at 5-second intervals could be extracted, no audio.**
**Strong opening:** "Basis: frames sampled every 5 s; no audio available. Timestamps are ±5 s, speech and music cannot be assessed, and brief events between samples may be missed. Here is what the frames show..."
